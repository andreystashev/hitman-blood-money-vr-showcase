# Roadmap

Stability and comfortable campaign play come first. No release dates are promised.

- Expand mission-action, container, outfit and dialogue regression coverage.
- Verify scoped firing/recoil and sight entry/exit across both hands and upgrades.
- Improve weapon support grips, arm posing and body visibility.
- Continue work on elevators, traversal cameras and moving-platform physics.
- Measure frame times on dense missions and improve rendering consistency.
- Expand OpenXR and non-Meta headset testing.
- Refine physical reloads, magazines and body storage before recommending them as defaults.
- Improve physical melee contact, sound and NPC reactions.
- Keep installation, controls and diagnostics approachable.

See [STATUS.md](STATUS.md) for current limits. Use issues for reproducible bugs and label feature requests clearly.
